BRIEFING · POSKO DAMKAR · 17:42 LOCAL

Wildfire north of the airstrip

  1. Take-off & PatrolStart the engines, flaps 1, take off from runway 36 and fly the 3 patrol rings along the river.
  2. First ScoopTouch down gently on the lake at 60–115 kt and skim until the tank is 80% full.
  3. Fire AttackFly through the target ring over the fire and hold SPACE. Refill and repeat until the main fire is down.
  4. Save the VillageThe wind turns east: lay a wet line between the fire and the stilt village before the timer ends.
  5. Return & LandFuel is low. Fly the approach gate, follow the PAPI lights, land on runway 36 and stop.

THREATS

  • SMOKEturbulence, low visibility, hot air that overheats the engines
  • ENGINESwatch ITT; an engine can catch fire. Press X to fire the bottle, then fly on one
  • BIRDShornbills over the canopy can strike the aircraft
  • STRUCTUREhard landings and high g damage the airframe
  • FUELlimited; a damaged tank leaks

Mission fails on a crash, empty tanks, a burned village or when the 25-minute mission clock runs out.
